Sources & Method

The Coffee Desk is an automated brief. Every figure on this page is fetched from the source named beside it and stamped with the time it was retrieved. Nothing is estimated, interpolated or filled in; where a source is unavailable the page says so.

Exchange prices are delayed and are shown for information only. This is not trading, investment or hedging advice, and it is not a substitute for your broker’s confirmations or the exchange’s own settlement data.
